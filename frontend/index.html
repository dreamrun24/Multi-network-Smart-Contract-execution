🧠 Your Smart Contract Code
📘 What is this contract?
This is a SimpleStorage contract that stores text on the blockchain. The setMessage() function saves your message, and getMessage() reads it back. Think of it like a digital sticky note that lives forever on the blockchain!

Edit the Solidity code. Deploy compiles your edits in-browser and deploys to the selected network.

Beginner Tip:
  • Change the line string private message = "Hello"; to your text, e.g. "Hi".
  • Click Step 4: Deploy Contract. We deploy exactly what’s in the editor.
  • After deploy, the box under Setup & Test shows Stored message: your text.
On testnets, connect your wallet first. In simulation mode, no wallet is required.
